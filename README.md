# Stickies!

> Your notes, wherever you browse.

Stickies is a Chrome extension that lets you create sticky notes directly on webpages and keep them with you as you browse.

Built for research, studying, troubleshooting, documentation, and anyone who spends a lot of time working in their browser.

## ✦ What is Stickies?

Ever find yourself switching between a webpage and a notes app just to write down one small thing?

Stickies keeps your notes where the context lives.

Create a note directly on a webpage, move and resize it however you like, and come back to it later. Notes can stay pinned across pages, while the Side Panel gives you a central place to browse and manage everything you've saved.

### Core features

- **Sticky Notes** — Create notes directly on any webpage.
- **Global Pinning** — Keep important notes visible as you move between webpages.
- **Side Panel** — Browse and navigate all your saved notes from one place.
- **Drag & Resize** — Position notes wherever they make sense on the page.
- **Color Coding** — Use different note colors to organize your thoughts.
- **Text Formatting** — Format notes with basic text styling.
- **Persistent Notes** — Your notes stay available when you return to them.
- **Floating Island** — Quick access to Stickies without getting in the way of your workflow.

## ✦ Built for the browser

Stickies is designed around a simple idea:

**Your notes should live where you're doing the work.**

Whether you're:

- researching a topic
- studying
- troubleshooting a technical issue
- reviewing documentation
- comparing products
- working through tickets
- collecting information
- or simply trying to remember something

Stickies lets you leave a thought exactly where you need it.
